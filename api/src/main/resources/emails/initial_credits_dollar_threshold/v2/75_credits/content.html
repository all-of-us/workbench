When your credits are exhausted:
  • You will not be able to launch new analysis environments.
  • Existing workspace resources will become inaccessible.
  • You will lose the ability to access, duplicate, or move workspace contents.
  • Workspace storage resources will be deleted.
  • The Researcher Workbench Support Team will be unable to recover your work.
Researchers should establish billing before their credits reach $0.
Recommended next steps
To avoid disruptions, you should take the following action before your credits expire.
  • Update your existing initial credits billing pod with a valid GCP billing account to preserve your existing workspaces.

    or

  • Create a new billing pod linked to a valid GCP billing account and duplicate your workspace(s) to that pod.
